## Easy and productive Kotlin development

Koin keeps you focused on your app, not on your tools.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Ready for Android" icon="rocket">
    Retrieve your dependencies in Android components with `by inject()` and `by viewModel()`, and in Jetpack Compose with `koinViewModel()`. See [Android](/docs/platforms/android/) and [Compose](/docs/platforms/compose/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Powering Kotlin Multiplatform" icon="puzzle">
    Share your modules across Android, iOS, desktop and web, with a platform module where a target needs its own code. See [Kotlin Multiplatform](/docs/platforms/kmp/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Server side with Ktor" icon="laptop">
    Inject into your routes and scope instances to a request. See [Ktor](/docs/platforms/ktor/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Productive and safe" icon="approve-check">
    Your classes stay plain Kotlin with constructor parameters, and the Koin Compiler Plugin checks your graph at build time. See [Compile-time safety](/docs/tooling/compile-time-safety/).
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Install

The recommended setup is the Koin BOM with the Koin Compiler Plugin, which needs Kotlin 2.3.20 or later. Here it is for Android. Each [platform guide](/docs/get-started/install/) lists the artifacts it needs.

<Install artifacts={['koin-android']} plugin bom />

For Koin Annotations, also add `koin-annotations`.

The plugin reports a missing dependency when you build, not when a user opens the screen. See [Koin Compiler Plugin](/docs/tooling/compiler-plugin/).

## Open source and community driven

<Image src={koinByKotzilla} alt="Koin by Kotzilla" width={220} class="koin-by-kotzilla" />

[Arnaud Giuliani](https://x.com/arnogiu) created and released Koin in 2017, and still maintains it with the help of open source [contributors](https://github.com/InsertKoinIO/koin/graphs/contributors). Koin is released under the Apache 2.0 license and is supported by [Kotzilla](https://kotzilla.io), with contributions from individuals and companies around the world.

Get help and follow the project on [GitHub](https://github.com/InsertKoinIO/koin), [Stack Overflow](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/tagged/koin) and the [Kotlin Slack](https://kotlinlang.slack.com/).
